ABOUT ME

I’ve been fascinated by how movies are made since I was four, when I watched the behind-the-scenes of The Amazing Spider-Man on my nan’s old TV. From then on, every trip to the cinema sent me searching YouTube for more. That led me to Bruce Lee, then to Jackie Chan’s My Stunts DVD, and soon I was roping my friends into making fight scenes with me, learning how action is built shot by shot.



Around the same time, I started making lego stop motion films inspired by YouTubers like Edbound and Aubreystudios88. Using a clean plate to paint out supports and make my figures jump was my first taste of VFX, and it grew into masking, rotoscoping and compositing. After learning composition, writing and camera work in school media, I taught myself colour grading and lighting to strengthen my stories, and now I’m learning virtual production and CGI.

Blue Journey



This is my Year 12 graduation film. My goal was to create a distinct atmosphere that added depth to my characters’ broken dynamic. I worked with my peers Harry and Millar to develop the script and bounce ideas around. I had a clear picture in my head of how many of the shots would look, so the next step was working out how to create them.


Shot lists and storyboards kept all of us on the same page visually. To get the look, I bought a Black Pro-Mist filter to soften highlights and create bloom while preserving the blacks. Shooting in RAW gave me a wider dynamic range and enough headroom to build a strong colour profile in DaVinci Resolve.


Despite our small team, the shooting schedule was disrupted when Millar, who had shot a small portion of the film, had to go away on holiday. Our mate Sonny stepped in to film the rest, and we pulled together to rework the story for a tighter schedule. The core story stayed the same, but the introduction lost some character development. It taught me to plan for the unexpected and build flexibility from the start.

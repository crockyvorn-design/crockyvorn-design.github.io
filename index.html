ABOUT ME

I’ve been fascinated by how movies are made since I was four, when I watched the behind-the-scenes of The Amazing Spider-Man on my nan’s old TV. That curiosity led me to "Jackie Chan’s My Stunts" DVD, and soon I was roping my friends into shooting fight scenes with me.


Making lego stop motion films introduced me to VFX, by using clean plates to paint out supports. This grew into masking, rotoscoping and compositing. Since then I’ve taught myself colour grading and lighting, and I’m now learning virtual production and CGI.

ABOUT ME

My fascination with how films are made started when I was four, watching the behind-the-scenes of The Amazing Spider-Man on my nan's old TV. From then on, every time I came home from the cinema I went looking for how the film was made. That curiosity led me to Bruce Lee, then to Jackie Chan's My Stunts DVD, and soon I was roping my friends into shooting fight scenes, learning how action is built shot by shot.

ABOUT ME

I’ve been fascinated by how movies are made since I was four, when I watched the behind-the-scenes of The Amazing Spider-Man on my nan’s old TV. That curiosity led me to "Jackie Chan’s My Stunts" DVD, and soon I was roping my friends into shooting fight scenes with me.


Making lego stop motion films introduced me to VFX, by using clean plates to paint out supports. This grew into masking, rotoscoping and compositing. Since then I’ve taught myself colour grading and lighting, and I’m now learning virtual production and CGI.






The following slides are projects i've worked on

|Signorinella: Little Miss|


In 2024 I did work experience with Resolution Media on their documentary Signorinella: Little Miss, which premiered at the Melbourne International Film Festival (MIFF). I filmed behind-the-scenes content and worked as camera assistant on one of the interviews, watching professionals work first-hand. Director Shannon Swan also had me edit parts of the interview scenes, and I was credited as camera assistant.


Year 12 Graduation Film |

Director and colourist


This is my Year 12 graduation film, made with my peers Harry and Millar, where I used a Black Pro-Mist filter and RAW footage graded in DaVinci Resolve to build a soft, atmospheric look that deepens my characters’ broken dynamic. When our cameraman went away mid-shoot and our mate Sonny stepped in, we reworked the story for a tighter schedule, which taught me to build flexibility into a plan from the start.



Year 11 Fight Scene Film |

Director and coordinator


This Year 11 previs project tested my eye for timing and coordination, inspired by Jackie Chan’s clear, readable action. My actors had no stunt experience, so I choreographed around what they could perform safely and built the pacing in the edit. I used 2D VFX to remove a wire and the people pulling me on it, but I accidentally left mats and lights in frame, which made me far more conscious of what’s visible in my Year 12 film.


Year 11 Horror Short |

Director


This Year 11 horror short follows a character who finds a video camera holding footage of the previous owners’ ghostly experiences. I used 2D VFX to make cups move by masking out wires, and built a fake early 2000s movie forum so the footage felt true to its time period. With a very tight schedule I learnt to organise shoot days carefully, and Harry and I painted a giant pentagram on a tarp as our biggest practical effect.

|3D CGI VFX|


I wanted to integrate CGI into live footage using only a phone, so I shot in 4K, captured an HDRI of the scene with an HDRI app instead of a 360 camera, and 3D scanned the surfaces that catch light, like the floor or grass, using Polycam. The 3D trackers I tried, even in industry software, weren’t reliable enough, so I built my own tracking workflow using GLOMAP. In Blender, I aligned the scan to the footage and used the HDRI to give the CGI object accurate reflections.
